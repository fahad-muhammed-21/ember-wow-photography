Terms & Conditions

Booking terms crafted for clarity, care, and a seamless experience

These terms apply to all bookings, custom quotes, shoot planning, and delivery arrangements with Ember Vow Weddings. Please read them carefully before reserving your date.

Our promise

Professional coverage, transparent planning, and respectful communication.

We work with couples, families and creative teams to deliver beautiful and honest coverage that feels personal, polished and aligned with your celebration. Our aim is to make the process clear from booking through gallery delivery.

Quick overview

  • Bookings are confirmed once the advance payment is received.
  • Final payment is due before the event date.
  • Travel, food, and accommodation for the crew are billed to the client.
  • Edited files are delivered by gallery and are available for a limited time.
  • Any additional working hours beyond the agreed schedule are charged as per the quote.

Terms and conditions

Expenses

Raw Files

Raw (non-edited) videos will not be provided.

Booking

Payment

Full payment must be settled before the wedding date.

Timings

Clients must strictly follow scheduled shoot timings; delays may affect coverage and deliverables.

Work beyond the scheduled timeframe will incur an additional charge of 250 AED per hour.

Function agenda must be provided at least 2 weeks prior to the event.

Documentary

A 30-minute documentary video is not part of any package (available at an additional charge).

Music

Client's preferred songs must be announced in advance; otherwise, the company will select the songs.

File Delivery

Edited files will be available in an online gallery for 3 months only; clients must download and back up files within this period.
