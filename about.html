About Ember Vow Weddings

Every part of your story, captured beautifully and authentically

A Dubai-based wedding photography and cinematography studio, built around one idea: your moments deserve to be relived, not just remembered.

Our story

Behind every frame is a dedicated creative crew

Ember Vow Weddings was built on a simple belief a wedding is a collection of emotions, heartfelt moments, laughter and tears that deserve to be remembered for a lifetime. Our passion is preserving those moments so you can relive the feeling of your day, again and again.

We've carefully curated a range of photography and cinematography packages to suit different celebrations, requirements and styles, and we build each crew around your specific function agenda from the smallest candid moment to the grandest celebration.
